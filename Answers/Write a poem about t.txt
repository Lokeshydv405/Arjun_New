The stars, they twinkle and shine,
A celestial show, so divine.
Like diamonds in the midnight sky,
They sparkle and gleam, catching the eye.

Their gentle glow, a guiding light,
For those who wander, lost in the night.
A path of hope, a beacon bright,
To lead us home, to the morning light.

Oh, stars of wonder, so pure and bright,
You dance across the endless night.
With every twinkle, a tale is spun,
Of secrets kept, and mysteries begun.

In the depths of space, you shine so fair,
A cosmic tapestry, beyond compare.
A reminder of the magic that's there,
A reminder of the wonder we share.

The stars, they whisper secrets low,
Of worlds beyond our own, and roads untold.
They speak of dreams, and hopes, and fears,
And the endless possibilities that fill our years.

Oh, stars of wonder, we reach for thee,
And the magic you bring, sets our souls free.
In your celestial show, we find our place,
Among the stars, in the vast, dark space.
